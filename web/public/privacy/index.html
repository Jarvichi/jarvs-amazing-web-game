Privacy Policy

Jarv's Amazing Web Game · last updated 29 August 2026

Jarv's Amazing Web Game is a browser and mobile card game. This page explains exactly what the game stores, who processes it, and how to get rid of it. It covers the game at jawg.uk and the Android and iOS builds of the same game.

The short version. You can play without an account. If you make one, we store your email address, your save file, your chosen player name and your leaderboard scores. We do not sell anything to anyone, there is no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK is embedded. You can delete everything from inside the game.

Playing without an account

You do not have to sign up. Without an account, your progress is kept only in your device's local storage and never leaves the device, except that the game signs you in anonymously to Firebase so leaderboards and shared features work. An anonymous sign-in has an internal ID but no email, name or other identifying information attached.

What is stored when you create an account

DataWhyWhere
Email address and account ID To sign you in and restore your save on another device Firebase Authentication
Save file — collection, deck, crystals, campaign progress, statistics Cloud backup and cross-device sync, when you press Sync Cloud Firestore
Player name To reserve the name and show it on leaderboards Cloud Firestore
Leaderboard entries — player name, score, date To rank daily, weekly, endless and minigame results Cloud Firestore
Feedback you submit, and your account ID with it So we can read and act on bug reports and suggestions Cloud Firestore
Crash and error reports, which may include your account ID To find and fix bugs Rollbar

What other players can see

Your player name is public. It appears on the daily, weekly, endless and minigame leaderboards alongside your score, and in the in-game news feed when you win a jackpot or find a rare card. Choose a name you are comfortable showing to other players. Nothing else you enter — your email address, your feedback, your save file — is ever shown to another player.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or attribution SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or behavioural tracking.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for anyone else's marketing.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data is requested.
  • Players cannot upload photos or files. The game's image uploads are reachable only by the developer account when posting news items.
  • Rollbar's session replay, which records on-screen activity to diagnose crashes, is switched off on phones and tablets and on the mobile app builds. It runs only on desktop browsers.

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — accounts and stored game data. Firebase privacy
  • Rollbar — crash and error reporting. Rollbar privacy
  • GitHub Pages — hosting for the web version, which receives ordinary web-server request logs.

How long it is kept

Account and save data is kept until you delete your account. Crash reports are kept by Rollbar under its own retention schedule and age out automatically. Leaderboard entries for a past day or week remain as a historical record unless removed with your account, as described below.

Deleting your account and data

In the game, open Settings → Account → Delete account. You will be asked for your password to confirm. This permanently removes your account, your cloud save, your player name reservation and your leaderboard entries, and clears the game's data on that device. Your email address is then free to register again.

One honest limitation: leaderboard entries recorded before the release that added this feature were not stored in a way that can be traced back to an account, so a small number of older scores may remain under the player name you were using. If you would like those removed, email us and we will do it by hand.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. The in-game delete covers erasure; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the game will note it in the in-game news feed.
